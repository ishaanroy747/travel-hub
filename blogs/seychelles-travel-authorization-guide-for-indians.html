The Seychelles Travel Authorization Guide for Indians: Steps, Fees & Approval Times

Understand the Seychelles Travel Authorisation process for Indian travellers, including documents, application steps, fees, processing options and arrival requirements.

Home   /   Blogs   /   Seychelles Travel Authorization

Seychelles is one of the most attractive island destinations for Indian travellers looking for beaches, clear waters, luxury resorts and a relaxed international holiday. But before booking your trip, there is one important entry requirement to understand.

Indian passport holders do not need a conventional tourist visa to enter Seychelles. However, all visitors must obtain an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before travelling. The ETA is a mandatory pre-travel authorisation and should not be confused with a traditional visa. A Visitor's Permit is issued on arrival when the traveller meets the applicable entry requirements.

Because many travellers still search for terms such as Seychelles Tourist Visa for Indians , this guide uses that phrase while explaining the current ETA process accurately.

Important: Seychelles does not require a conventional tourist visa for Indian passport holders, but travellers must complete the required Travel Authorisation before departure and meet the applicable immigration conditions.

Do Indians Need a Seychelles Tourist Visa?

No conventional visa is required for Indian passport holders visiting Seychelles for tourism.

Instead, travellers must complete the Seychelles Travel Authorisation process before departure. The official Seychelles Electronic Border System states that visitors can apply for their Travel Authorisation within 30 days of their scheduled arrival.

This distinction matters because a Travel Authorisation is not the same as a visa approval.

Once you arrive in Seychelles, the Visitor's Permit is issued by immigration if you meet the entry conditions, including having:

  • A valid passport
  • A confirmed return or onward ticket
  • Confirmed accommodation
  • Sufficient funds for the duration of the stay
  • The required Travel Authorisation before departure

The initial Visitor's Permit can be issued for up to three months, subject to the applicable conditions.

What Is the Seychelles Travel Authorisation?

The Seychelles Travel Authorisation is a digital pre-travel screening and immigration requirement.

The official Electronic Border System is used to submit traveller information before the journey. The system asks visitors to provide passport information, a traveller photograph, trip details and other required information before making the applicable payment.

The process is fully online, which means Indian travellers do not need to visit an embassy simply to submit a conventional tourist visa application.

It is still important to complete the process correctly because having a Travel Authorisation does not remove the other entry conditions imposed by Seychelles immigration.

Seychelles Travel Authorisation Fees

The official Seychelles Tourism Department has previously stated that the standard Travel Authorisation application fee is €10 per application.

The current electronic system also provides different processing options, including Standard, Premium and Expedited processing. The amount payable can therefore depend on the selected service.

Travellers should check the amount displayed in the official system at the time of application rather than relying on an old fee quoted online.

This is particularly important because private travel-service providers may charge their own assistance or documentation fees in addition to the government Travel Authorisation charge.

Seychelles Travel Authorisation Processing Time

Unlike a conventional tourist visa, the Seychelles Travel Authorisation is a pre-travel electronic authorisation.

The official system currently allows travellers to choose from different processing options, including Standard, Premium and Expedited processing.

For this reason, travellers should not treat a third-party advertised processing time as a guaranteed government approval period.

A safer approach is to complete the Travel Authorisation well before the flight, allowing time to correct an application if additional information or documents are requested.

Documents Required for Indians

The exact information requested can depend on the traveller and trip, but the official Seychelles system currently requires visitors to provide key travel information and supporting details.

You should be prepared with:

  • Valid Indian passport
  • Recent traveller photograph or selfie
  • Flight or travel details
  • Accommodation information
  • Contact details
  • Payment card for the applicable fee
  • Travel insurance covering the stay

The Seychelles Ministry of Foreign Affairs also lists travel information, certified accommodation details and valid travel insurance with full medical coverage for the duration of the stay among the information required for Travel Authorisation applications.

Additional information may be requested depending on your circumstances.

How to Apply for Seychelles Travel Authorisation from India?

The application is completed online.

Step 1: Confirm Your Travel Plans

Have your intended arrival date, departure information and accommodation details ready before starting the application.

Step 2: Prepare Your Passport and Photograph

Upload the passport photo page and provide the required traveller photograph. The official system specifically asks applicants to scan or upload the passport photo page and take or upload a traveller photo.

Step 3: Enter Your Travel Information

Provide the requested flight, accommodation and personal information accurately. Make sure your dates and booking information correspond with your actual travel plans.

Step 4: Complete the Required Questions

The application may include health and customs questions. Answer them accurately rather than making assumptions about what information is relevant.

Step 5: Pay the Applicable Fee

Complete payment using the accepted card option and select the available processing service according to your needs.

Step 6: Track Your Application

After submission, you receive a reference number that can be used to check the application status through the electronic system.

Step 7: Keep the Authorisation Accessible

Once approved, keep a digital copy available on your phone and retain your other travel documents for the journey.

What Happens After You Reach Seychelles?

The Travel Authorisation is not the final entry decision.

On arrival, Seychelles immigration assesses whether you satisfy the conditions for a Visitor's Permit.

The official requirements include a valid return or onward ticket, confirmed accommodation and sufficient funds for the intended stay. The current immigration guidance specifies a minimum of US$150 per day or equivalent as the stated financial requirement.

The initial Visitor's Permit can be granted for up to three months. Extensions are possible under certain conditions, but they are subject to immigration approval.

Seychelles Tourist Visa for Indians: Important Planning Tips

Although the process is simpler than applying for a traditional sticker visa, travellers should still prepare carefully.

Don't confuse ETA with a visa

When searching for Seychelles Tourist Visa for Indians, you will find many websites using the word “visa.” The current official terminology is Travel Authorisation/ETA, while the Visitor's Permit is issued on arrival.

Apply within the permitted window

The official system states that visitors can apply for Travel Authorisation within 30 days of their scheduled arrival.

Keep accommodation details ready

Accommodation information forms part of the Travel Authorisation and entry process.

Carry proof of onward travel

A valid return or onward ticket is one of the stated Visitor's Permit requirements.

Don't rely on outdated fee information

Fees and processing options can change. Check the amount shown when you submit your application.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even though Seychelles does not require a traditional tourist visa for Indian travellers, mistakes in the pre-travel process can still create complications.

Avoid:

FAQs About Seychelles Tourist Visa for Indians

Do Indians need a tourist visa for Seychelles?

No. Indian passport holders do not need a conventional tourist visa, but they must obtain the required Electronic Travel Authorisation before travelling.

Is Seychelles visa-free for Indians?

Seychelles does not require a conventional visa for Indian passport holders. However, visa-free entry does not mean travellers can arrive without preparation. A Travel Authorisation, accommodation, return/onward ticket and sufficient funds are among the requirements that apply.

How much does Seychelles Travel Authorisation cost?

The standard Travel Authorisation fee has been stated by Seychelles authorities as €10 per application. Processing options and charges should be checked in the official system at the time of application.

How long can Indians stay in Seychelles?

The initial Visitor's Permit can be issued for up to three months, provided the traveller satisfies the applicable entry conditions.

Can I apply for the Seychelles ETA before booking everything?

You need relevant trip information for the application, including travel and accommodation details. The official system allows applications within 30 days of the scheduled arrival date.

Does ETA approval guarantee entry into Seychelles?

No. The Travel Authorisation is a pre-travel requirement. Entry remains subject to immigration checks and the conditions for obtaining a Visitor's Permit.

Is travel insurance required?

The Seychelles Ministry of Foreign Affairs lists valid travel insurance with full medical coverage for the entire stay among the Travel Authorisation requirements.

Final Thoughts

For Indian travellers, the Seychelles entry process is relatively straightforward once the difference between a tourist visa, Travel Authorisation and Visitor's Permit is understood.

The most important step is completing the required ETA before departure and keeping your passport, accommodation, return or onward ticket, insurance and financial arrangements ready for immigration checks.

If you are searching for a Seychelles Tourist Visa for Indians, remember that the current process is based on Travel Authorisation rather than a conventional tourist visa.

Checking the latest official requirements shortly before your trip is essential because fees, processing options and entry procedures can change.
