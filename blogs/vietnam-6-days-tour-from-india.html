How to Plan a 6-Day Vietnam Trip from India: Itinerary, Visa & Travel Guide?

Explore a practical six-day Vietnam itinerary covering Hanoi and Halong Bay, along with visa information, travel planning tips, packing advice and local experiences.

Vietnam is a practical choice for Indian travelers who want an international holiday that combines culture, natural scenery, local food, historic places, and modern cities. With a well-planned route, six days are enough to experience several of the country's major highlights without trying to cover every region.

A Vietnam 6 Days Tour Package can be structured around Hanoi and Halong Bay, with enough time for sightseeing, local experiences, and relaxation. For first-time visitors from India, this duration works particularly well when the itinerary is planned around a few key destinations instead of moving between too many cities.

Why Choose Vietnam for a 6-Day Holiday from India?

Vietnam offers a strong mix of experiences within relatively short travel distances. Hanoi introduces travelers to Vietnamese history and everyday city life, while Halong Bay provides a completely different setting with limestone islands and scenic waters.

For Indian travelers, a six-day trip can also be easier to manage than a longer multi-city holiday. Instead of spending most of the vacation in transit, you can focus on meaningful sightseeing and leave some time for exploring local markets, trying Vietnamese cuisine, and enjoying the atmosphere of each destination.

When comparing different Vietnam Tour Packages, check whether the itinerary leaves enough time for sightseeing rather than simply adding more destinations to the schedule.

Suggested 6-Day Vietnam Itinerary

The following plan is suitable for travelers looking for a balanced Vietnam 6 Days Tour Package from India.

Day 1: Arrive in Hanoi

Start your Vietnam holiday by flying into Hanoi. After completing arrival formalities, transfer to your hotel and settle in.

Keep the first evening relatively relaxed. Depending on your arrival time, you can walk around the Old Quarter, visit a nearby market, or enjoy your first Vietnamese meal.

Avoid planning major sightseeing immediately after a long international flight. A slower first evening makes the rest of the itinerary easier to manage.

Day 2: Explore Hanoi

Spend the second day discovering Hanoi's cultural and historical side. Depending on your interests and available time, your sightseeing can include important landmarks, traditional markets, historic areas, and local streets.

The Old Quarter is particularly useful for experiencing the city's atmosphere. You can also try local dishes and explore small shops before returning to your hotel.

For travelers researching Vietnam Tour Packages for families or couples, Hanoi is a useful starting point because it combines sightseeing, food, shopping, and cultural experiences in one destination.

Day 3: Travel to Halong Bay and Enjoy a Cruise

Day three is dedicated to Halong Bay, one of Vietnam's most recognizable natural attractions.

Travel from Hanoi toward the bay and board your cruise. The experience generally focuses on scenic views, limestone formations, onboard meals, and time on the water.

A Halong Bay cruise is one of the main highlights of a Vietnam 6 Days Tour Package, particularly for first-time visitors. Keep your camera ready, but also allow time to simply enjoy the scenery rather than rushing from one activity to another.

Day 4: Return to Hanoi

After breakfast and the remaining cruise activities, return toward Hanoi.

Use the evening for flexible plans. You can revisit an area you enjoyed earlier, explore a local café, shop for souvenirs, or simply rest at your hotel.

Keeping this evening flexible is useful because travel times can vary depending on traffic and cruise schedules.

Day 5: Leisure and Optional Experiences

Day five can be customized according to your interests.

You could spend more time exploring Hanoi or choose an optional excursion based on your travel preferences. Families may prefer relaxed sightseeing, while couples could focus on cafés, markets, photography spots, and local experiences.

This flexible day is one reason a carefully designed Vietnam 6 Days Tour Package can work better than an itinerary that attempts to cover too many cities.

Day 6: Departure

After breakfast, complete your hotel check-out and transfer to the airport for your return flight to India.

Keep your passport, boarding documents, e-visa, and other travel documents easily accessible until you leave Vietnam.

Vietnam Visa Requirements for Indian Travelers

Indian passport holders planning a tourist trip to Vietnam need to arrange the appropriate visa before travel. Vietnam's official immigration system currently provides an electronic visa option for eligible foreign travelers.

The official immigration portal states that Vietnam e-visas can be valid for up to 90 days and can be issued for either single or multiple entry. Applicants outside Vietnam can apply electronically using the official system.

For a six-day holiday, travelers should carefully check the information entered in the application before submitting it. The official application process requires passport information, a portrait photograph, passport data-page image, intended entry information, and other travel details.

Before applying:
  • Make sure your passport meets the validity requirement.
  • Enter your name and passport details exactly as shown on your passport.
  • Upload the required photograph and passport data page correctly.
  • Select the intended entry and exit points carefully.
  • Check your approved e-visa before departure.
  • Keep a digital copy and a printed copy with your travel documents.

The official immigration portal currently states that the standard processing period is three working days, but travelers should avoid applying at the last possible moment.

Visa rules and processing requirements can change, so Indian travelers should verify the latest requirements on the official Vietnam immigration portal before submitting an application.

How to Reach Vietnam from India

Vietnam can be reached from several major Indian cities through direct or connecting flights, depending on the current airline schedule.

Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City are the country's major international gateways. For a six-day North Vietnam itinerary, Hanoi is generally the more logical arrival point because it places you closer to the first destinations in the plan.

When booking Vietnam Tour Packages , check whether international airfare is included or needs to be arranged separately. Also compare arrival and departure timings because an inconvenient flight schedule can reduce the usable sightseeing time during a short trip.

Best Time to Plan a Vietnam Trip

Vietnam has different weather patterns across its regions, so the ideal travel period depends on the destinations included in your itinerary.

For a Hanoi and Halong Bay-focused trip, travelers should check the expected weather before confirming their dates. The reference package page identifies October to April as a popular period for sightseeing, while May and September are described as shoulder-season months. June to August can bring higher humidity and rainfall in parts of the country.

If your dates are fixed, do not build the entire trip around weather assumptions. Instead, check the forecast closer to departure and keep some flexibility in outdoor activities.

What Indian Travelers Should Pack

Packing for six days does not require a large suitcase. Focus on practical clothing and items that suit your itinerary.

Consider carrying:

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Lightweight clothes suitable for warm weather
  • A light jacket for cooler evenings
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
  • A compact umbrella or rain protection
  • Universal travel adapter
  • Basic medicines and personal essentials
  • Passport, visa documents, flight tickets, and hotel confirmations

If your itinerary includes a cruise, keep one set of comfortable clothes and essential items easily accessible rather than packing everything into your main luggage.

Food and Local Travel Tips

Vietnamese cuisine is an important part of the travel experience. Indian travelers can try popular local dishes while also finding Indian restaurants in major tourist areas.

Carry some Vietnamese Dong for smaller purchases, especially at local markets and smaller establishments. Cards are more commonly accepted in hotels and larger businesses, but having local currency available is useful.

English is commonly encountered in tourist-oriented areas, although communication can be more limited in smaller local establishments.

Most importantly, keep your travel documents secure and respect local customs when visiting religious and cultural sites.

How to Choose the Right Vietnam Tour Package

Not every itinerary advertised as a six-day holiday provides the same experience. Compare the actual day-by-day schedule before making a decision.

Look for:

  • Number of hotel nights included
  • Destinations covered
  • Airport and local transfers
  • Halong Bay cruise arrangements
  • Sightseeing inclusions
  • Meals mentioned in the itinerary
  • Visa assistance, if offered
  • Flexibility for customized activities
  • Hotel category and location

A useful Vietnam 6 Days Tour Package should have a realistic pace. Adding Hanoi, Halong Bay, Da Nang, Hoi An, Ho Chi Minh City, and the Mekong Delta into six days may look attractive on paper, but excessive transfers can leave very little time for actually experiencing each place.

For first-time visitors, concentrating on North Vietnam can make the trip more manageable.

Conclusion

Planning a six-day Vietnam holiday from India becomes much easier when you select a focused itinerary, arrange the visa before departure, and allow enough time for both sightseeing and rest. Hanoi and Halong Bay provide a strong combination of cultural exploration and natural scenery without requiring constant movement between destinations.

Travelers can also compare different Vietnam Tour Packages and select an itinerary based on their preferred pace, hotel category, activities, and travel dates.

A reliable Tour Operator in India can also help coordinate accommodation, transfers, sightseeing, and other travel arrangements when you prefer a planned holiday rather than organizing every component separately.

With the right preparation, six days can provide a well-rounded introduction to Vietnam while leaving you enough time to enjoy the destination instead of rushing through it.
