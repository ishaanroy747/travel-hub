7-Day Cyprus Itinerary for Indians: Visa Tips & Nicosia Sights

Planning a week in Cyprus from India can be a great way to combine Mediterranean scenery, historic cities, local food and relaxed coastal days without trying to cover the entire island at once. But before booking hotels or building your itinerary, Indian travellers should understand the visa requirements and plan their route carefully.

Quick answer: A seven-day Cyprus holiday can provide a practical introduction to the island, especially when you combine Nicosia with Larnaca, Limassol and either Paphos or a beach-focused destination.

Cyprus Visa for Indians: What Should You Know?

For most ordinary Indian passport holders, a Cyprus Visa for Indians is required before travelling to the Republic of Cyprus. Cyprus currently lists India among the countries whose nationals need a visa for entry.

However, there are important exceptions for certain travellers who already hold qualifying Schengen visas or European residence permits.

If you're planning your first Cyprus trip, this seven-day itinerary offers a practical balance between Nicosia's history, coastal experiences and some of the island's most appealing destinations.

Typical Cyprus Visa Documents

Depending on your circumstances, your application can require:

  • Completed and signed visa application form
  • Valid passport with at least two blank pages
  • Recent passport photograph
  • Proof of accommodation
  • Flight reservation
  • Travel medical insurance
  • Evidence of sufficient financial resources
  • Employment, education or residential-status documents
  • Supporting documents explaining the purpose of your trip

The Cyprus High Commission in New Delhi states that travel medical insurance should cover the stay and provide minimum coverage of €30,000. It also notes that applicants may be asked for additional documents.

Applications in India are currently handled through the Cyprus visa process involving the High Commission and its external service provider arrangements. The New Delhi High Commission says applications are by appointment, while VFS Global services have been outsourced for a six-month period beginning July 21, 2026.

The official processing guideline is generally up to 15 days, although individual cases can take longer, potentially up to 45 days when additional scrutiny is required. Therefore, don't leave your visa application until the last minute.

A Practical 7-Day Cyprus Itinerary for Indians

A week is enough to experience several sides of Cyprus if you avoid changing hotels every night. The following route gives you a mixture of culture, beaches, sightseeing and relaxed travel.

Day 1: Arrive in Cyprus and Settle In

Begin your Cyprus holiday with arrival and hotel check-in.

If your flight arrives in Larnaca, use the first day to settle into your accommodation rather than immediately planning a packed sightseeing schedule.

Spend the evening walking around the local area, enjoying a Cypriot dinner and getting familiar with the surroundings.

Keep your passport, accommodation details, travel insurance and other important documents accessible during your trip.

Day 2: Explore Nicosia's Historic Centre

Dedicate your second day to Nicosia, also known as Lefkosia.

Nicosia is particularly interesting because its historic centre combines Venetian walls, museums, religious buildings, traditional streets and modern city life.

Start around the old city and explore:

  • Venetian walls
  • Ledra Street
  • Famagusta Gate
  • Laiki Geitonia
  • Cyprus Archaeological Museum
  • Leventis Municipal Museum
  • Byzantine Museum
  • Archbishopric area
  • Omeriye area

The official Nicosia map also identifies attractions such as Ledra Street, Famagusta Gate, the Cyprus Museum, Leventis Municipal Museum and several historic churches and museums.

Day 3: Nicosia at a Slower Pace

Use your third day for the parts of Nicosia that deserve more time.

The Medieval Walls of Lefkosia were constructed by the Venetians in the 16th century and feature eleven heart-shaped bastions, making them one of the city's most recognisable historic features.

You can also explore:

  • Ledra Museum Observatory
  • Cyprus Handicraft Centre
  • Ethnographic Museum
  • Omeriye Baths
  • Local cafés and traditional restaurants
  • Contemporary art galleries

For travellers interested in history and culture, this can be one of the most rewarding parts of the trip.

Day 4: Larnaca and the Coast

Move towards Larnaca for a change of scenery.

Spend time around the waterfront, explore the city's historic areas and enjoy a relaxed seaside lunch.

Avoid packing too many attractions into one day; Cyprus works particularly well when you leave some room for spontaneous stops.

If you're travelling during warmer months, keep sightseeing around the cooler parts of the day and leave the afternoon for a slower coastal experience.

Day 5: Limassol Day Trip

Head towards Limassol for your fifth day.

Explore the old town, marina and coastal areas before taking some time to enjoy local food.

Limassol offers a different atmosphere from Nicosia, combining historic areas with a modern seaside setting.

This is also a good day to keep your schedule flexible. Depending on your interests, you could spend more time exploring the city or simply enjoy the Mediterranean atmosphere.

Day 6: Paphos or a Beach-Focused Day

For Day 6, choose between sightseeing in Paphos and having a relaxed beach day.

Paphos is a strong choice if you want more history and archaeology.

If your priority is relaxation, select a nearby coastal area instead and enjoy a slower final full day.

Cyprus Tourism officially promotes the island for its combination of culture, beaches, nature, gastronomy and outdoor experiences, so you don't need to turn every day into a sightseeing marathon.

Day 7: Final Morning and Departure

Keep your last day simple.

Have breakfast, complete your check-out and allow enough time for the airport transfer.

If your flight is later in the day, use the morning for a final walk or meal rather than adding a distant attraction.

A relaxed final day can also help avoid unnecessary travel stress.

How Much Time Should You Spend in Nicosia?

For first-time visitors, two days is a comfortable amount of time for Nicosia.

The official Cyprus Tourism itinerary for Nicosia recommends a combination of the walled city, Laiki Geitonia, Ledra and Onasagorou streets, the Cyprus Archaeological Museum and other cultural attractions.

If you're especially interested in history, museums and architecture, you can easily spend longer exploring the capital.

Cyprus Visa Planning Tips for Indian Travellers

A successful visa application isn't just about submitting documents. Your overall travel plan should make sense.

Before applying, make sure:

  1. Your passport meets the validity requirements.
  2. Your itinerary matches your accommodation bookings.
  3. Your financial documents support the planned trip.
  4. Your employment or business situation is properly documented.
  5. Your travel insurance covers the complete stay.
  6. Your flight and hotel reservations are consistent with your itinerary.
  7. You apply well before your intended departure.

Important: The Cyprus authorities specifically advise applicants not to purchase travel tickets before the visa has been issued.

Cyprus Visa vs International Tourist Visa: What Is the Difference?

An International Tourist Visa is a broad term used for travel visas required by different countries.

A Cyprus tourist visa, however, is specifically issued under Cyprus's own entry rules.

One important point is that Cyprus should not simply be treated as another Schengen destination.

Cyprus government information states that, until full integration into the enhanced Schengen cooperation area, Cyprus visas are valid for Cyprus and do not themselves provide free movement throughout the rest of the Schengen area.

This distinction matters when planning a wider Europe itinerary.

FAQs About Cyprus Visa for Indians

Do Indians need a visa for Cyprus?

Yes. Ordinary Indian passport holders generally require a Cyprus visa before travelling. However, certain travellers holding qualifying Schengen visas or European residence documents may be exempt from obtaining a separate Cyprus national visa.

How long can I stay in Cyprus on a short-stay visa?

A Category C short-stay visa permits a maximum stay of up to 90 days within the applicable 180-day period, subject to the conditions of the visa issued.

Can a Schengen visa be used to enter Cyprus?

A valid double- or multiple-entry Schengen Category C visa can allow entry into Cyprus without a separate Cyprus national visa, subject to the applicable requirements.

How early should Indians apply for a Cyprus visa?

Applications can generally be submitted up to six months before the intended trip and should be submitted at least 15 calendar days before arrival.

Since some cases can take longer, applying early is advisable.

Is seven days enough for Cyprus?

Yes. Seven days can provide a good introduction to Cyprus, especially if you combine Nicosia with Larnaca, Limassol and either Paphos or a beach-focused destination.

Final Thoughts
